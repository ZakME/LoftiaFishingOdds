How the odds work

  • Each list rolls once. Percentages are each entry's weight out of the list's total.
  • Striped bars give two of the item.
  • Pairs like “Atlantic Salmon / Sturgeon” are one entry with several fish. The data doesn't say how the game picks between them.
  • Every catch also gets separate bonus rolls for district coordinates, cosmetics and adventure nodes.

Spot facts

  • Spots respawn 30 seconds after they're fished.
  • Rare spots are capped at 3 per spawn group, every other tier at 5. Within a group each tier has the same spawn weight.
  • Rare spots show the game's “Unique” effect. The separate “Rare” effect isn't used by any spot.
  • Ore spots only appear in caves.
